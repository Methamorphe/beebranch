# BeeBranch 1.8.1

The pipeline detail now reads like the forge's: stages, their jobs, and the job log without leaving the application.

## Added

- **Stage detail and job logs**: each stage of a pipeline shows its own aggregated state, followed by its jobs with their status and duration. Opening a job reads its log in place, with paging for a long trace and the link that opens the full job on the forge. The pipeline and its jobs remain reachable from the navigator's Pipelines section, next to the commit indicator.

## Notes

- The update artifact is signed with the same key as 1.8.0: Settings → Updates offers 1.8.1, and “Return to the previous version” goes back to 1.8.0 through `rollback.json`.
